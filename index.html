01 / ROBOTICS

7-DOF Bimanual Mobile Manipulation

ROS 2 · Gazebo

Simulated mobile-manipulation pipeline spanning perception, localisation, navigation, collision-aware planning, bimanual grasping, reorientation, and placement.

  • Developed a closed-form 7-DOF inverse-kinematics solver with elbow-swivel selection.
  • Demonstrated collision-free 90° object reorientation with 13–14 mm clearance and 1–4 mm final object drift.
  • Built a 166-check validation harness; mutation testing detected 12 of 13 injected faults.

Robot Dynamics Solver

Python · Numerical Methods

Standalone N-DOF rigid-body dynamics library using both Lagrangian mechanics and Featherstone algorithms.

03 / COMPUTER VISION

Metal Surface Defect Classification

CNN · TensorFlow/Keras

Custom lightweight CNN for six classes of grayscale metal-surface defects, evaluated with stratified, duplicate-grouped 5-fold cross-validation.

  • 305K-parameter model trained and evaluated on 1,800 images.
  • 99.06% ± 0.87% mean accuracy across folds; analysed class-wise confusion between visually similar defect categories.
  • Created a single-image inference tool returning predicted labels and top-k class scores.

04 / APPLIED ML

Energy Optimisation in Metal Manufacturing

Physics-informed ML

Simulation-based CNC milling energy analysis combining a fitted physics-based power model, explainability, residual anomaly detection, and constrained optimisation.

  • Achieved R² = 0.93 and MAPE = 3.3% on unseen simulated jobs.
  • Designed a cycle-time-constrained optimiser reporting 37% mean simulated energy savings.
  • Exposed predictions and simulated telemetry through FastAPI, AWS IoT Core (MQTT), and Streamlit.

The data and reported energy savings are simulation-based, not measured production-floor outcomes.
